**Author:** Scott Idler
**Date:** 2026-11-18
**Status:** Implemented
**Review Passes Completed:** 3/5 (Draft, Correctness, Edge Cases); panel rounds 1 and 2 folded

## Summary

- Build `voice-guard`, a mod beside `rails` and `session`, that carries the `emdash` rule at `tool.call` plus a table of new AI-tic bans from the 2026-11-18 scour. As built, the `prose.sh` rules and the offer rule stay in `prose.sh`: Phase 0 recorded Q1 and Q4a NEGATIVE, so the mod carries no `classic.Stop` or `classic.SubagentStop` surface (Phases 3 and 4 dropped).
- The mod sits ABOVE the settings hooks in the hook chain. So the migration runs in two modes: shadow (the mod judges, logs, and always passes through; the hook still decides) then enforce (the mod decides; the hook entries are removed).
- The scripts stay in the repo, unwired, for every entry that is retired. Hooks that are not voice guards are untouched.
- Out-of-band doc: no chunk of `docs/design/2026-09-13-setup-audit-program.md` is opened or advanced. The baton has no chunk marked `next`.

## Problem Statement

### Background

- Voice is enforced three ways today:
  - Mechanical: `emdash.sh` (PreToolUse deny of U+2014, 4 matchers) and `prose.sh` (Stop and SubagentStop, 5 rules, 2 entries).
  - Routed text: `routed/voice.md`, injected by the `rails` mod.
  - Advisory: `~/Claude/writing/VOICE.md` anti-pattern list, `edges.md`.
- The 2026-09-12 audit measured the lesson already: a written rule alone failed (em-dashes in 31% of September turns under a ban in 14 files). Only hooks held.
- The 2026-11-18 scour found about 30 new tics worth banning. None is enforced, and "belt and suspenders" was added to VOICE.md as text only.
- Hooks predate the mod API in Claude Code 2.1.327. A mod can hold one TypeScript table and one test suite instead of bash regex split over two scripts.
- Prior art, abandoned: `prose.sh:233-240` says rules 4 and 5 were "Ported from the reply-guard mod (2026-11-15): a prompt.compose mod is skipped by the org guard cc-plugin-sec-default". No reply-guard source exists in this repo.

### Problem

- New bans have no enforcement path short of editing bash.
- Two scripts, 976 lines with their tests, carry the voice rules in bash, awk, and jq.
- Hook chain order makes a naive "run both" unsafe: `[managed settings hooks, ...hooks modules, the other settings hooks as core]` (`claude-code.d.ts:1563-1564`), and `$.tool.call` raises `classic.PreToolUse` beneath every plugin's `tool.call` hook (`reference.md:130`). A mod hook that answers without calling `next(e)` ends the chain, so every settings hook beneath it never runs. That includes `hindsight-retain.sh` on Stop and the non-voice PreToolUse guards.
- A mod fails open (a throw, a bad shape, a timeout, or a failed load skips the hook).

### Goals

- One phrase table, one pure-function matcher module, one test suite, enforced at the tool boundary (outward prose) and at turn end.
- Port `emdash.sh` and the four regex rules of `prose.sh`; port the offer rule with its instruction test.
- Prove parity in shadow mode before any hook entry is removed, rule by rule and surface by surface.
- Ship the new bans warn-only first, measured through the production extraction before any rule denies.
- A disabled mod is visible at SessionStart, and a mod that failed to load is visible at the start of the next session.

### Non-Goals

- Excluded: the non-voice guards (`git-release-guard`, `secret-echo-guard`, `terraform-apply-guard`, `intent-guard`, `slack-post-guard`, `pr-title-guard`, `jira-ac-guard`, the rest). `slack-post-guard.sh` checks target, test-text, and resend (`slack-post-guard.sh:7-30`), never prose style.
- Excluded: auto-rewriting text. `turn.complete` cannot rewrite the shown answer (see Resolved Decisions).
- Excluded: editing `VOICE.md`, or copying the private profile into this public repo.
- Excluded: `prompt.compose` injection. It is skipped on this machine by `cc-plugin-sec-default` (the reply-guard finding). `rails` injects through `prompt.submit` and `tool.call` context.
- Parked, revisit if Phase 0 proves them: `MessageDisplay` display-only rewrite (not enforcement); reading `VOICE.md` through `$.fs` at runtime.

## Proposed Solution

### Overview

- New plugin folder `HOME/.claude/skills/voice-guard/`, laid out like `rails` and `session`: `.claude-plugin/plugin.json`, `hooks/hooks.json` (`{"modules":["./index.ts"]}`), `hooks/index.ts`, `hooks/rules.ts`, `spec/` (bun), `tests/` (`claude plugin test`), `types/`.
- `manifest.yml` links `HOME/.claude/skills` whole, so no manifest entry is needed. The folder loads as `voice-guard@skills-dir`. It has no `SKILL.md`, so it is not scanned as a skill.
- Hook surfaces (as built):
  - `tool.call` (matchers as in `emdash.sh`).
  - `turn.complete`: warn-only text beneath the answer, for the structural bans.
  - `session.start`: writes the heartbeat and prunes old heartbeats and logs.
  - `classic.Stop` and `classic.SubagentStop` are removed: Q1 NEGATIVE (a user-tier `classic.Stop` block does not run under `cc-plugin-sec-default`), so both `prose.sh` entries stay.

### The chain invariant

- Every pass path calls `next(e)` and returns its result. A match judges before `next`.
- Shadow mode (`enforce: false`, the default): the mod judges, then `const r = await next(e)`, logs `{ modVerdict, chainResult }` side by side, and returns `r`. The hook beneath decides. This is how parity is observed without the hook keeping any log of its own: `emdash.sh` keeps none (`emdash.sh:79-83`), and `prose.sh` logs only offer decisions (`prose.sh:362`, `:365`).
- `chainResult` mapping: when a settings hook denies a tool call, the call ends errored, with the reason as its `text` (`claude-code.d.ts:19276-19278`), not as a `{ deny }` value. The mod maps an errored result that carries a hook's deny text to `chainResult: deny`, a Stop `{ block }` result to `chainResult: block`, and anything else to `allow`. Phase 0 Q6 records the exact shapes before the mapping is coded.
- Enforce mode (`enforce: true`): on a match the mod returns `{ deny }` or `{ block }`, which ends the chain. On a pass it still returns `next(e)`.
- Each phase that adds a surface carries a `claude plugin test` case asserting a settings hook beneath still runs on a clean input.

### Architecture

- `rules.ts`: pure functions, `(text) -> Violation[]`. No `$`, no I/O, so `bun test` covers them like `rails/spec`.
- `index.ts`: wires `rules.ts` to the surfaces; owns the matcher-to-field map: `Bash.command`, `Write.content`, `Edit.new_string`, `MultiEdit.edits[].new_string`, `NotebookEdit.new_source`, and any string at any depth in an `mcp__*` input.
- Failure policy: every `.catch` fails open (`next(e)`), matching `emdash.sh:48-49` ("A guard that cannot read its input must not wedge the session") and `prose.sh`'s pass-throughs. A throwing rule writes a `catch` row to the mod's log.
- Rule tiers, per rule id:
  - `block` (ported, same text): em-dash, UTC without Pacific, unlinked PR numbers, decision ask over 40 lines, offer-closer.
  - `deny-outward` (new, regex-safe, outward writes only: Slack, Jira, Confluence, Marquee, `create_pr`, commit and `gh` bodies): `belt and suspenders`, `belt-and-braces`, Q-number and AC-number references, chat residue (`Great question`, `Certainly`, `You're absolutely right`), staged run-ups (`Let's dive in`, `without further ado`, `Honestly?`, `Real talk`), `Let that sink in`, `Full stop.`
  - `warn` (new, structural, never blocked: a false positive costs a Stop retry): `not only/just X but Y`, trailing `-ing` significance tails, triads, copula avoidance (`serves as`, `stands as`), bold-label bullets, `Here's X:` lead-ins, arguing with no one (`To be clear`, `I'm not saying`), writing about the document, `ensure/ensuring`, `promote/foster/streamline/facilitate`, `essential/crucial/important` as emphasis.
- New bans apply to outward prose only, matching `routed/voice.md` Non-triggers (code, code comments, terminal replies to Scott, scratch notes). Terminal replies get `warn` only.
- Offer-closer instruction test (`prose.sh:282-302`): slash prompt is always an instruction; else one yes/no to TypeSafe `jev-1.13.0` (threshold 0.5, 3s timeout); else the VERBS fallback (`prose.sh:106`). The judge in the mod is `$.http.fetch` to the same Jev endpoint when Phase 0 shows it is reachable (same judge, so parity by construction). `$.model.classify` is the fallback only: it returns a label or `undefined`, not a probability (`claude-code.d.ts:3083-3100`), so the 0.5 threshold and the log's `p` field do not carry over; the log records `p: null` and `source: "classify"`. Either call runs against a `$.clock` race that supplies the 3s timeout, because the hook budget stops while any `$` call is in flight (`claude-code.d.ts:6095-6097`).

### Data Model

- `Rule = { id, tier: 'block' | 'deny-outward' | 'warn', pattern, scope, fix }`. `fix` is the recast instruction the model sees.
- Mod log: one file per session and per module copy, `~/.local/state/voice-guard/<sessionId>.<copyTag>.jsonl`, the same scheme as `rails`' router log (`rails/hooks/index.ts:1156-1172`). Row fields: `prose.sh`'s plus `{ surface, rule, modVerdict, chainResult, mode }`. `$.fs.write` writes whole files only, so two writers on one file lose each other's lines: sessions that run at once, a hot reload that loads a second copy, or `prose.sh`'s own `>>` append would each lose rows. Past a row cap the oldest rows go, as in `rails`. The mod never writes `prose.sh`'s file. `bin/prose-report` is taught to read `prose.sh`'s file and the glob in Phase 6.
- Heartbeat: `~/.local/state/voice-guard/heartbeat.<sessionId>`, written at `session.start` (`claude-code.d.ts:5198-5212`: once per process for each loaded plugin). `plugin.register` is not used: it judges the modules that join after this one, not this one's own load (`claude-code.d.ts:5326-5338`). The mod keeps the newest 20 heartbeat files and prunes session logs older than 14 days through `rkvr rmrf`, as `rails` prunes its router logs.
- Log row field `chainSource` (`settings` | `plugin` | null): who answered beneath the mod, so the probe matrix counts only `settings` rows against the hooks. Null when nothing denied or the mod itself denied.
- The `MultiEdit.edits[].new_string` selector is inert: Claude Code 2.1.327 has no `MultiEdit` tool. It is kept so the matcher map matches `emdash.sh`.

### API Design

- Plugin config under `pluginConfigs["voice-guard@skills-dir"].options`: `enforce` (boolean, default false), `deny_outward` (a space-separated string of rule ids promoted to deny; default `""`; the manifest schema rejects an array, though the reader also accepts one), `warn_structural` (boolean, default false). No hyphens in keys.

**Model:** sonnet

## Resolved Decisions

- 2026-11-18, correction of an earlier claim in this session: `turn.complete` cannot rewrite or block the answer. Its doc comment reads "Return `{ text }` with a different text to show it beneath the answer (a synopsis, a TL;DR line); the transcript's record is never rewritten" (`claude-code.d.ts:5362-5370`). Block-and-reprompt is `classic.Stop` returning `{ block }`. In-place rewrite exists only at `tool.call`, and this doc does not use it.
- Panel round 1, 2026-11-18, the mod sits above the hooks (`claude-code.d.ts:1563-1564`). "Hook and mod both wired" is therefore not two independent guards. The shadow and enforce modes and the chain invariant replace it. The double-block worry in the first draft is withdrawn: classic results fold into one per event (`claude-code.d.ts:1591-1593`) and a mod block ends the chain before `prose.sh` runs. The risk that replaced it is bypass.
- Panel round 2, 2026-11-18: both seats accepted the two pushbacks (no hook-plus-mod double block; the phrase table stays in `rules.ts`), so both round-1 findings are withdrawn. The fold's own errors are corrected here: `chainResult` mapping from an errored result, the continuation table and phase gates made consistent, Q2b keeping all 4 `emdash.sh` entries, the heartbeat moved to `session.start` with a read-compare-write preflight and per-session files, per-session log files, parity probes limited to ported rules, and the Jev replay floor applying to every judge.
- Fail open everywhere, matching `emdash.sh:48-49` and `prose.sh`. The first draft's fail-closed `.catch` on `tool.call` was a behavior change from the hook it ports; it is dropped.
- `rails` is precedent for `tool.call` and `prompt.submit` only (`rails/hooks/index.ts:1867-1880`), not for `classic.Stop`. Q1 is the gate.
- Em-dash handling stays deny-and-recast, not rewrite.
- Structural bans never block.
- The phrase table lives in `rules.ts` as code. Pushback on moving it to a JSON file read through `$.fs`: a skills-folder plugin hot-reloads on save (`reference.md:121`) and `manifest.yml` links the folder whole, so editing `rules.ts` costs what a JSON edit would, and the table carries fixtures and tests. The private profile stays out of this public repo. Each rule names its VOICE.md bullet or public source. Scott's own corrections are paraphrased, never quoted.
- Rollback order after Phase 7: restore the removed settings entries first, then disable the plugin. Disabling alone leaves no em-dash guard.
- The mod writes its own per-session log files, never `prose.sh`'s.
- `prose.sh` and `emdash.sh` stay in the repo, unwired, for each entry that is retired.
- Name `voice-guard`.
- Build close, audit round 3, 2026-11-18: the as-built state is folded into this body (surfaces, Phases 3 and 4 dropped, `deny_outward` as a string, `chainSource`, inert MultiEdit selector, acceptance checkboxes). Findings fixed in code: a false "did not load" warning after `/clear` (preflight now skips `source: clear`), per-session log prune at 14 days, a `session.start` pass-through case, and the env-var `prose-report` test. Deferred: `turn.complete` returns `{ ...r, text }`, which replaces any `text` a module beneath set; no other mod hooks `turn.complete` today.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: Keep hooks, add regexes to `prose.sh`
- **Description:** extend the bash scripts with the new bans.
- **Pros:** no new failure mode; the guards work; tests exist.
- **Cons:** bash regex and `jq` for 30 rules; no shared table; each rule costs fixtures in two scripts.
- **Why not chosen:** the table is the point. This stays the fallback for the Stop half if Q1 is NEGATIVE.

### Alternative 2: Consolidate every guard into one mod
- **Description:** move the release, secret, terraform, and intent guards too.
- **Pros:** one codebase and one test runner.
- **Cons:** a failed load fails open, and those guards protect irreversible actions. The mod also sits above them, so a pass path that skips `next(e)` silently disables them.
- **Why not chosen:** parked until Phase 6's load check has run in production. A separate design doc.

### Alternative 3: `prompt.compose` injection of the ban list
- **Description:** put the full ban list in the system prompt every session.
- **Pros:** the model sees every rule.
- **Cons:** skipped on this machine by `cc-plugin-sec-default` (reply-guard, 2026-11-15); the audit showed prose rules fail without a hook.
- **Why not chosen:** negative prior result, re-measured once in Phase 0 Q4.

## Technical Considerations

### Dependencies

- `bun` (used by `rails/spec`), `claude plugin test`, `claude plugin validate --strict`.
- `ESCOTE_TYPESAFE_API_KEY` and the Jev endpoint for the judge (existing dependency of `prose.sh`).
- Managed settings: `cc-plugin-sec-default` is seated outermost (`docs/design/2026-10-08-rule-routing.md:268-270`) and lists `classic.*`. Q1 decides whether a user-tier `classic.Stop` runs.

### Blast Radius

- This repo only: `HOME/.claude/skills/voice-guard/`, `hooks-preflight.sh` and its test, `.otto.yml`, `bin/prose-report`, `settings.json` (Phase 7).
- No cross-repo change. `VOICE.md` lives in `scottidler/writing` and is untouched.
- Ship order: Phases 0-6 land with the hooks deciding (shadow mode). Phase 7 is the only change that removes protection, and it is an operator step behind a probe matrix.
- Bypass risk: a mod hook that does not call `next(e)` silently disables every settings hook beneath it, `hindsight-retain.sh` and the non-voice guards included. The chain invariant and the per-phase clean-input test are the mitigation.
- Failure mode: the mod fails open on throw, bad shape, timeout, or failed load (`reference.md:128`). After Phase 7 a failed load leaves the voice rules unenforced until the next session's preflight names it.

### Performance

- No subprocess per call (hooks spawn bash and `jq` each time). The Jev call is rare: it runs only after the offer regex matches.

### Security

- The mod runs in the managed-settings environment. The Jev key is read from env, never logged.
- No secret enters the mod folder. Run `mcp__plugin-authoring__scan_secrets` on it before any publish.

### Testing Strategy

- `bun test` for `rules.ts`; `claude plugin test` for hook wiring and the clean-input pass-through cases; golden fixtures ported from the two `*-test.sh` files; the Phase 4 replay; the Phase 5 hit and false-positive counts; the Phase 7 probe matrix.
- `claude -p "reply OK"` from a scratch dir after any startup-config change.

### Rollout Plan

- Phases 0-6, then Phase 7. Rollback: restore the removed settings entries, then set `enabledPlugins["voice-guard@skills-dir"]` to false.
